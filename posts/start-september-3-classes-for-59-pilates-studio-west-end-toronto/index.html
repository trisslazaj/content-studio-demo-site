Start September with 3 Classes for $59 at Our Pilates Studio in West End Toronto

Three classes to get you started

The September schedule is live, so here is the simplest way in: our intro offer, 3 classes for $59.

The pass is valid 30 days from the day you buy it. Three classes across a month is an easy thing to fit around a job — one a week, more or less, with room to move them around.

You can use it on any class except the members-only Sunday Open Studio. Reformer, mat, whatever suits the day you have free. (Hi, team NAVI.)

What to expect when you arrive

We're at 118 Wrenfield Ave, Unit 3, Toronto, ON M6P 2X9. Unit 3 — worth noting before you're standing out front.

Doors open 15 minutes before class. That's your window. There's no entry once the warm-up has started, and we do mean that one; it isn't a mood, it's the rule. If you're running behind, better to take the next class than to try your luck.

Mats, props and springs are provided, so you don't need to carry anything in. The studio has 8 reformers and 12 mats.

The one thing you do need: grip socks are required on the reformers. If you don't own a pair, we sell them at the desk for $18.

September schedule updates

The September schedule is up now. Eleven classes on it, plus one addition.

We've added a second Prenatal Mat class, Saturdays at 11:30am, starting from week two. So if the existing prenatal slot never worked with your week, there's now a Saturday option.

A quick summary of what's changed:

  • New: Prenatal Mat, Saturdays at 11:30am, from week two of September.
  • Otherwise: 11 classes on the schedule.
  • Not on the intro pass: Sunday Open Studio, which is members-only.

If you're returning after a summer of not much, three classes in 30 days is a reasonable place to restart. If you're brand new, it's enough to find out whether reformer or mat suits you better, without committing to either.

Book your first class

Buy the 3-class intro offer and book your first session on the September schedule — reformer or mat, whichever one you fancy. Bring grip socks if you're on a reformer, and give yourself the 15 minutes before class.
