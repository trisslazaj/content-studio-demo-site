Wrapping Up July: Pack Discounts, Sold-Out Workshops, and More Prenatal Classes

2026-09-22

A busy July in Wrenfield Village

Almost every 6:30am Reformer Flow class ran full this month, which is not something we expected from a pilates studio in west end Toronto in July. Usually people are at the cottage. This year they were on the reformers at 6:30 in the morning, which we respect enormously.

Marco's Breath & Core workshop on July 18 also filled all 12 spots. If you were on the fence and then found it booked out, that's our fault for not telling you sooner. We'll give more notice next time.

For anyone new here: we're at 118 Wrenfield Ave, Unit 3. We run 11 classes a week on 8 reformers and 12 mats. That's the whole operation. It means classes stay small and we actually learn your name and which shoulder gives you trouble.

Last call for the July Reset

The July Reset promotion ends July 31 at 11:59pm, and we're not extending it.

Use the code RESET20 for 20% off a 10-class pack. That takes it from $290 to $232. The pack is good for six months from the date you buy it, so if you're heading out of town in August, you're not losing anything by stocking up now — you've got until roughly the end of January to use them.

This is the part where a bigger company would write something about investing in yourself. We'll just say: pilates class packs are cheaper than drop-ins, and the discount goes away at midnight on the 31st.

More prenatal mat classes in August

Priya's Saturday 9:00am Prenatal Mat is currently the busiest class we run on a Saturday. It has been for a while now, and the waitlist stopped being occasional and started being every week.

So starting the second week of August, we're adding a second Prenatal Mat class on Saturdays at 11:30am. Same format, later start, which we suspect a few of you will prefer anyway.

If you teach prenatal pilates in Toronto and this sounds like your kind of room: we're looking to hire a certified instructor to co-lead Saturday morning prenatal classes. Get in touch. We'd rather find the right person than rush it.

Lunch breaks and private sessions

Not everyone can get here at 6:30am, and we'd never suggest you should. For the midday crowd, Marco teaches a 30-minute Lunch Express Reformer class on Tuesdays at 12:15pm.

Thirty minutes is enough to do real work and still get back to your desk without a full costume change. It's become a reliable little fixture, and it's the class we point people to when they say they don't have time for a lunchtime pilates class. You do, as it turns out. Just barely.

If group classes aren't the right fit — you're managing an injury, you're pregnant and want something built around you, or you just learn better one-to-one — private reformer sessions with Dana or Marco are $95 for 60 minutes. A lot of people do one private at the start to get the setup and the springs sorted, then move into classes feeling less lost.

Studio reminders: socks, doors, and parking

A few practical things, mostly for new faces but a refresher never hurts:

  • Doors open 15 minutes before class. Come early if it's your first time — there's paperwork and we'll walk you through the machine.
  • No entry once the warm-up has started. This isn't us being precious. The springs are loaded and people are mid-movement, and a late entry at the wrong moment is a genuine safety problem.
  • Grip socks are required on the reformers. If you forget yours, we sell them at the desk for $18. We know. Bring your own next time.
  • Parking: there's free two-hour street parking on Wrenfield Ave, which is usually fine outside of Saturday mornings. If the street is full, there's a paid lot behind the bakery at 122 Wrenfield Ave for $3 an hour.

Saturdays are the tight one for parking, especially now that we'll have two prenatal classes running. Give yourself an extra ten minutes and you'll be fine.

Ready to start?

July told us a few useful things. Mornings are busier than we thought. Workshops sell out faster than we plan for. And Saturday prenatal needed more room, so it's getting more room.

August looks like a good month to start if you've been meaning to. The reformers stay cool, the classes are small, and nobody here is going to make you do anything alarming in week one.

So: grab the July Reset 10-class pack with code RESET20 before it expires on July 31, or if you're new to the studio, book the 3-class intro offer — three classes for $59, valid for 30 days. That's enough to find out whether reformer pilates is your thing without committing to anything.

See you at 118 Wrenfield Ave. Bring socks.
